Namibia Golf Federation is supporting our favorite team and trying to build a loyal fan base that knows about the sport. Our commitment is to strengthen the image of our team and our partners, seeking to become the best club in the country.

Our systems are reliable and well-equipped spaces for our associates enjoy an open place in which to practice your favorite sport every day, easily accessible and within the city. We invite you to learn more about our services and the benefits of membership of the club, here on our website.

Feel free to contact us if you have a question about our products or need for personalized service.
